# iKubb — First users & monetization strategy

**Date:** 2026-07 (peak season) · Strategy only — no build items.

## 1. Who the first users actually are

The app is a **companion to a physical product people already own** (a
€20–40 wooden game sold at garden centres, toy stores, bol.com,
Decathlon). That defines everything: nobody searches "kubb app" from the
couch — they need it *mid-game, outdoors, in a group*.

| Segment | Context | What they need | App fit today |
|---|---|---|---|
| Families & friend groups (25–55, NL/BE/DE/Scandinavia) | Garden, BBQ, beach; kids playing along | Instant start, no rule arguments, no mental math | Quick start, "needs exactly X", rules panel — strong |
| Campers (NL camping culture, ACSI/ANWB crowd) | Campsite fields, games borrowed from reception | Rules they don't know, offline reliability | Tour + bundled fonts/offline — strong |
| Club & tournament players (France is the giant: the FFM/Mölkky federations; Finland's world championships) | Organized play, score desks | Scoreboard display, stats, later brackets | Scoreboard mode + stats — good; brackets = v2 pro feature |

**Seasonality is extreme** (April–September in Europe). It is July now:
every week of delay costs a meaningful share of the year's acquisition
window. France + Finland + Benelux are the beachhead markets — the 10
store-listing languages are already an asset.

## 2. The app through their eyes (honest review)

- **The value moment is mid-game.** Acquisition must happen *before* or
  *at* that moment — at purchase, or when someone Googles the rules
  mid-argument. App-store browsing will never be the top of funnel.
- **The rules content is the acquisition asset.** People Google
  "mölkky regels" / "règles mölkky" standing next to the pins. The app
  already contains illustrated, localized rules — republished as simple
  web pages linking to the app, this ranks easily (low competition) and
  converts at the perfect moment.
- **The share card is the built-in viral loop.** Every shared result
  lands in a group chat of people who just played — the exact audience.
  (Later polish: a subtle store link/QR on the card.)
- **Competitors are weak.** Existing Mölkky scorers are dated,
  English/French-only utility apps. Design, rules-teaching, and ten
  languages are real differentiation in a small pond — ranking #1 for
  "mölkky" keywords is achievable.

## 3. Finding the first 1,000 users (ranked, cheapest first)

1. **Clubs as evangelists (weeks 1–2).** Personally approach 5–10 clubs
   (French federation clubs, Dutch/Belgian kubb groups, Finnish Mölkky
   scene) + the big Facebook groups (Mölkky France is the largest).
   Offer TestFlight access; the scoreboard display mode is literally
   built for their tournament desks. Ask for feedback and, at launch,
   reviews. Ten clubs ≈ hundreds of highly-networked players.
2. **Rules SEO (week 1, near-zero cost).** Publish the rules content as
   static pages per language (nummerkubb-spelregels, règles du mölkky,
   Mölkky-Regeln…) with the app link. Evergreen, compounding, catches
   people at the exact moment of need.
3. **ASO in a low-competition niche.** Localized listings in all 10
   languages, keywords: mölkky, molkky score, nummerkubb, kubb spel,
   Wikingerkegeln. Screenshots per locale (pipeline exists). Free app +
   niche keywords + early club reviews = top ranking within weeks.
4. **Manufacturer/importer partnership (the asymmetric bet).** Pitch
   Tactic Games (Mölkky's maker) and NL importers/garden-centre chains:
   a free, beautiful companion app increases their product's value; a
   QR insert in the box or on the shelf display puts the app in every
   buyer's hands at purchase. One yes here outperforms all paid
   marketing. Fallback: printable QR scorecards for shops/clubs.
5. **Campsite channel (NL summer).** Campsite animation teams and game
   rental desks; ACSI/ANWB and camping-blog content ("de camping-klassieker,
   nu zonder ruzie over de telling"). A few campsite partnerships reach
   thousands of families in-context.
6. **Short-form video.** Trick shots + family moments are native to this
   game. Partner with 2–3 family/camping creators (NL/FR/FI) rather than
   producing yourself. Winter = pause; restart in April.

Skip: Product Hunt (wrong audience), paid UA (LTV can't carry it),
generic press.

## 4. Monetization review

| Model | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Paid upfront | ❌ | Kills the share-card viral loop and rules-SEO funnel; casual seasonal users won't pay to try |
| Subscription | ❌ (v1) | No recurring value story for a scoring tool; winter churn. Only viable later as club/tournament SaaS |
| Ads | ❌ | Poisons the premium Scandinavian brand; low eCPM at this scale |
| **One-time Pro unlock** | ✅ core | €4.99, Family-Sharing on. Matches spec §7 and the audience's buy-once mentality (they bought a wooden game once too) |
| Tip jar | ✅ minor | Costs nothing, fits the goodwill tone |
| Affiliate | ✅ sleeper | "Don't own the game yet?" link (bol.com/Amazon partner) — the rules-SEO pages will attract people researching the *purchase*; early on this can out-earn IAP |
| B2B/white-label | later | Co-branded tournament mode for federations/manufacturers once brackets exist |

**Sequencing:** launch 100% free (maximize reviews, rank, and the
club goodwill) → season 2 (spring 2027) introduce Pro gating only NEW
features (tournament brackets, deep stats, theme/avatar packs). Never
paywall anything users already have — loss aversion turns reviews sour.
Add the affiliate link to the web rules pages immediately.

## 5. Validation without building anything

1. Ten conversations: 5 club organizers + 5 campsite families (does the
   scoreboard desk mode fit tournaments? would families pay €5 for
   brackets/stats?).
2. Keyword sizing: Apple Search Ads suggested-bid volumes for
   mölkky/molkky/kubb per storefront (free signal of demand by country).
3. TestFlight waitlist posted in the two biggest Facebook groups —
   conversion of that post is the cheapest demand test available.
4. Track share-card taps once live: it's the proxy for the viral
   coefficient, the number that decides how much acquisition ever needs
   to be paid for.

## 6. This summer vs. next spring

Now (July–Sept): TestFlight to clubs, App Store launch ASAP, rules
pages live, manufacturer pitch sent. Winter: build Pro features
(brackets, deep stats) while traffic is low. March: Pro launch +
creator content + shelf-QR push, ready for the season.
